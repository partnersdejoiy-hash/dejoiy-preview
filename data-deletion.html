Data Deletion

Effective date: September 21, 2026 · DEJOIY India Pvt. Ltd.

Your right to deletion

You can ask DEJOIY India Pvt. Ltd. to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. This page explains exactly how.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at deepak.sharma@dejoiy.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your details — your full name and the phone number or email address you used with our services, so we can find your records.
  3. We confirm and delete — we will confirm receipt, delete your personal data from our systems within 30 days, and email you once done.

Prefer WhatsApp? You can also send your deletion request as a message to our official business number +91 87006 72329.

What gets deleted

  • Contact details you shared (name, phone number, email address).
  • Internship application details and enquiry records.
  • Message history with our support team, where we control it.

What we may need to keep

Some records must be kept for legal, tax, or accounting reasons (for example, invoices for completed orders). Where that applies, we keep only the minimum required by law and tell you so in our confirmation email.

Questions?

Email deepak.sharma@dejoiy.com — we reply to every deletion request personally.
