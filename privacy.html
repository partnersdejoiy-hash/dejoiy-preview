Who we are

DEJOIY India Pvt. Ltd. ("DEJOIY", "we", "us") runs an internship program, ecommerce services, and BPO services in India. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

WhatsApp messaging

When you message our WhatsApp business number, your messages are processed through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta handles that data under its own privacy policy in addition to ours. We use message content only to reply to you and provide our services.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary: with service providers who help us operate (for example, messaging or hosting providers), or when required by law.
